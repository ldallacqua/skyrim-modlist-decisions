# Changelog

Each version says whether it's safe for an existing save. Mods are marked **Added**, **Moved**, **Updated**, **Incorporated** or **Dropped**, the legend Step uses.

It puts sparse pebbles and small rocks on Northern Roads' dirt roads, which had no grass or debris at all. Decided in [0015](decisions/0015-keep-tomatorim-ground-and-northern-roads.md). Safe for an existing save. Not yet seen in game.

- **Added, then disabled:** Terrain Depth - Outputs, in `Outputs`, generated by the new `tools/terrain_depth.py`, and listed in `rules/rules.json` as an output mod. It deepened the PBR ground's parallax toward the converted road's, as the owner asked after a look in game. In game the ground smeared into streaks, so it was disabled the same day and the ground is back to TomatoRim's own maps. The folder is kept. See [the road edges note](research/road_edges_and_debris.md#deeper-ground-instead-of-a-shallower-road) and [0015](decisions/0015-keep-tomatorim-ground-and-northern-roads.md).
  - **What it holds:** 59 of the winning ground height maps, stretched around their own average toward a relief of 0.09, at most 3 times. Also 9 texture set files whose `displacementScale` rose toward 1.0 with the average re-centred: TomatoRim's pine forest 02 (0.325) and Reach grass (0.627), and seven of Faultier's Soul Cairn sets.
  - **Why not raise the scales:** height blending weighs layers by `(height - 0.5) * displacementScale`. Raising the scale alone would also lift the ground's average and let it swallow the road's edges and faint trails.
  - **Conflicts on purpose:** it wins over TomatoRim PBR Landscapes, TomatoRim PBR - Northern Roads, Faultier's PBR Skyrim, Tomato's Riften and Ratway and Vanaheimr Mines and Caves, on those files only.
  - **Why it smeared:** the files were as intended (averages kept, spread raised, every mip checked). TomatoRim's height maps carry little real height detail, and a 1.6 to 3 times stretch made their small bumps too steep for the shader's 16-step parallax march.
- **Changed:** the converted Omen Apollo road's `displacementScale` from 1.0 to 0.5, then back to 1.0 the same day. At 0.5 the owner found the road flat. 1.0 is the value TomatoRim's own files give this set. The ground stays TomatoRim's and the roads stay Northern Roads, rather than a move to Vanaheimr or back to Blended Roads ([0015](decisions/0015-keep-tomatorim-ground-and-northern-roads.md)).
- **Fixed:** Northern Roads' stone spike pillars and Reach bridges looked furry and smeared. PGPatcher then gave them TomatoRim's mountain material, made for whole mountains, with glint, parallax and 75° normal smoothing. Those four meshes are now hidden in MO2 (`.mohidden`). The Patches Compendium meshes win again, and PGPatcher gives them the PBR textures PBR Hub made for them. Found from the owner's screenshot. Not yet seen in game.

- **Added:** Northern Roads - Omen Apollo Dirt PBR (converted), at the top of `Landscape`. It's a PBR conversion, made here, of [Complex Parallax Texture for Northern Roads](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/93860) 4K 1.02 by Omen Apollo, for rougher, trampled dirt roads with deep parallax. Not yet seen in game.
  - **Why a conversion:** the original is an ENB complex parallax texture. TomatoRim's road plugin points the road texture sets at `textures\pbr\`, so the game never loads it. Community Shaders couldn't draw it next to PBR ground anyway. PGPatcher 2.3.1 converts only parallax to complex material, and only on meshes.
  - **The maps:** made by the new `tools/terrain_to_pbr.py`.

    - The height, from the colour map's alpha, became a 2K BC4 map.
    - The normal is kept at 4K.
    - RMAOS is 2K, with roughness 0.86 to 0.96, no metal and AO 1.
    - The 8 trail colour maps are copies of the base, as they are in the original.
  - **Texture set files:** copies of TomatoRim's 10 files for these sets, values unchanged, so the parallax depth can be tuned in this mod.
  - **Conflicts on purpose:** it wins over TomatoRim PBR - Northern Roads and PBR Hub - Northern Roads PBR, on 12 textures and 10 texture set files.
