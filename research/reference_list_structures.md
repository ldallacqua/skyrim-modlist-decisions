# Reference Skyrim SE modlist structures in Mod Organizer 2: primary evidence (as of 2026-11-02)

## What separators do the top lists use, in what order, and how many mods and plugins do they have?

### Takeaway
Real `modlist.txt` files were parsed for 25 lists through the Load Order Library API. Separator counts range from 13 to 289, with a median of about 22 mods per separator, so no single standard size exists. Nolvus, LoreRim, Lexy's, Elysium, Eldergleam, Gate to Sovngarde, Licentia NEXT and Wildlander are verified from files. Step is verified from its official guide headings plus one personalized upload. Viva Skyrim could not be verified at all.

### Cited Findings

#### Method and how far to trust the numbers
- Load Order Library exposes the actual uploaded files as JSON at `https://api.loadorderlibrary.com/v1/lists/<slug>`, including line-by-line `content` — [LOL API docs](https://docs.loadorderlibrary.com/); [example record](https://api.loadorderlibrary.com/v1/lists/lorerim).
- The API's `modlist.txt` content starts with the top of the MO2 pane and ends with the MO2 header line, so it is already in pane order. This was checked on the Nolvus 5.2.5, LoreRim and Elysium records, for example `CORE` and `Base Game, Unofficial Patches & Fixes` come first in LoreRim — [LoreRim record](https://api.loadorderlibrary.com/v1/lists/lorerim).
- Counting rules: "mods" excludes separators and `*` unmanaged DLC/Creation Club lines. "Sep." counts `_separator` entries. "Empty" counts separators with no mods directly below them. A mod belongs to the nearest separator above it.
- Plugins come from `plugins.txt` where uploaded, otherwise `loadorder.txt` (marked "LO"). Where a list has both, `loadorder.txt` runs about 80 lines longer in these records, for example Nolvus 5.2.5 at 2288 and 2367. These are upload counts, not official claims.
- Wabbajack "archives" come from each list's `download_metadata.NumberOfArchives`, reached through the [repositories index](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wabbajack-tools/mod-lists/master/repositories.json). Archives are download files, not mods, so they are not comparable to the mod column.
- The upload flag `verified` was true only for Wildlander (Phinocio), Wunduniik Reloaded (Alaxouche), Lost Legacy (GreatPadinski), Aurora (Phoenix) and Septimus (Guitarninja). Other uploads are identified by uploader name and website field only.

#### Official guides checked
- **Step 2.3:** the official guide's ModGroup headings run in this order: `02-Extenders`, `03-Resources`, `04-Foundation`, `05-Animation and Physics`, `06-Models and Textures`, `07-Sounds and Music`, `08-Character Appearance`, `09-Fixes`, `10-Gameplay-General`, `11-Gameplay-AI and Combat`, `12-Gameplay-Economy`, `13-Gameplay-Immersion`, `14-Gameplay-Quests`, `15-Gameplay-Skills and Perks`, `16-Interface`, `17-Locations`, `18-Lighting and Weather`, `19-Utilities`, `20-Patches`, `21-Post-Processing` — [Step SkyrimSE 2.3](https://stepmodifications.org/wiki/SkyrimSE:2.3).
- **Step 2.3 on separators:** "Step uses the guide ModGroup names, which are handy", mods sit alphabetically within each group, and any exceptions are intentional — [Step SkyrimSE 2.3](https://stepmodifications.org/wiki/SkyrimSE:2.3).
- **Nolvus:** the v5 manual guide chapters use the same outline as the separators, for example `5.6.1 Trees and barks` and `10 Outputs` with `10.1 Synthesis` through `10.7 MAPS` — [Nolvus guide navigation](https://www.nolvus.net/guide/awakening). Nolvus Awakening (v6) has no manual guide yet — [nolvus.net](https://www.nolvus.net/).
- **Lexy's:** the guide is organized as Mod Installation Parts 1-10, then Merge Page and The Finishing Line, which matches the upload's banners and final separators. Its homepage says both "over 1,500" and "over 1,400" modifications, and does not mention separators — [lexyslotd.com](https://lexyslotd.com/).

#### Exact separator sequences (from the files, top of pane first)
Names are quoted as they appear. Two display changes were made so the lists stay readable: long runs of `_` or `-` inside names are shortened to three characters and `…`, and the Gate to Sovngarde suffixes `===== ALL CONFLICTS WORK AS INTENDED (V115) =====` and `===== NO CONFLICTS =====` are removed.

## Do they number separators, and what naming and nesting schemes appear?

### Takeaway
Most lists do not number. Of 24 SE lists, 18 use no numbering, 5 number nearly everything and 1 numbers only its parents. Numbered lists are not better ordered by that fact alone: Wunduniik's numbers have drifted out of pane order. MO2 nesting is simulated with empty header separators or with name prefixes.

### Cited Findings
- **Decimal outline, no parent separators:** Nolvus uses `1.1 SKSE PLUGINS`, `5.6.2 GRASS` and `5.10.1 NPC's RESSOURCES`. There is no `5. VISUALS` separator; the hierarchy exists only in the numbers. It does add unnumbered `WHITERUN`, `SOLITUDE`, `RIFTEN`, `MARKARTH` and `WINDHELM` under `5.11.1 CITIES` — [Nolvus v6 upload](https://loadorderlibrary.com/lists/nolvus-v6-ultimate-open-beta-start).
- **Flat zero-padded:** Elysium runs from `00 ROOT FOLDER` to `46 RULE 11` — [Elysium](https://loadorderlibrary.com/lists/elysium-remastered-332). Tahrovin (VR) runs from `00 - Tahrovin V3.0.5` to `48 - Disabled Mods (leave these alone)` — [Tahrovin](https://loadorderlibrary.com/lists/tahrovin-4). Step's guide headings run from `02-Extenders` to `21-Post-Processing` — [Step](https://stepmodifications.org/wiki/SkyrimSE:2.3).
- **Decimal with parent and child:** Eldergleam uses `13 - ARCHITECTURE MESHES` followed by `13.1 - ARCHITECTURE - WHITERUN -` through `13.16 - ARCHITECTURE - CASTLE VOLKIHAR -` — [Eldergleam](https://loadorderlibrary.com/lists/eldergleam-a-next-gen-modlist-4-0-2).
- **Ruled parents plus decimals:** Wunduniik uses `01. ___ CORE MODS ___` with `01.1 EARLY LOADERS - CORE`. Its top-level order in the pane is 01, 02, 03, 06, 07, 05, 04, 08, and `04.7`, `16.1`, `16.2` and `16.5` each occur twice — [Wunduniik](https://loadorderlibrary.com/lists/wunduniik-chapter-v).
- **Numbered parents only, with reserved high numbers:** Licentia uses `1 - BASE` through `14 - LIGHNTING OVERHAUL`, then jumps to `98 - PATCHES` and `99 - LN CUSTOM PATCHES AND OUTPUTS`. Children such as `Weathers` are unnumbered — [Licentia NEXT](https://loadorderlibrary.com/lists/licentia-next).
- **Decorated empty headers, unnumbered:** Apostasy has `--- VISUALS ---` — [Apostasy](https://loadorderlibrary.com/lists/apostasy). Living Skyrim has `---------- ║ NATURE ║ ---…` — [Living Skyrim](https://loadorderlibrary.com/lists/living-skyrim-5). Twisted has `~~AUDIO~~` — [Twisted](https://loadorderlibrary.com/lists/twisted-skyrim-4). LoreRim and CSVP use plain capitals such as `CORE` — [LoreRim](https://loadorderlibrary.com/lists/lorerim).
- **Path-style names:** Gate to Sovngarde repeats the full path, such as `Exterior Overhauls - Major Cities - Whiterun - Patches & Add-ons`, and appends `===== ALL CONFLICTS WORK AS INTENDED (V115) =====` or `===== NO CONFLICTS =====` to record conflict status — [GTS v115](https://loadorderlibrary.com/lists/gate-to-sovngarde-ae-wabbajack-conversion).
- **Decoration without numbers on every separator:** Lexy's wraps every category as `++++Weather Systems++++` and uses `MOD INSTALLATION PART 2` as a stage banner. Part 1 is spelled `MOD INTALLATION PART 1` in the file — [Lexy's](https://loadorderlibrary.com/lists/lexys-lotd-a-skyrim-modding-guide).
- **Bare `[NOLVUS] CATEGORY` style:** this form did not appear in any Nolvus file parsed; Nolvus uses the decimal outline above — [Nolvus v6 upload](https://loadorderlibrary.com/lists/nolvus-v6-ultimate-open-beta-start).
- **Operational instructions in separator names** are common: `ENB Options (ONLY PICK 1)` and `DO NOT DISABLE - DUMMY PLUGINS` (LoreRim), `FWMF MAPS (MUST LOAD LAST)` (Eldergleam), `Licentia NEXT - Bodyslide Output - Needs to always stay at the end` (Licentia), `Enable for Rudy ENB` (Halls of Sovngarde) — [LoreRim](https://loadorderlibrary.com/lists/lorerim); [Eldergleam](https://loadorderlibrary.com/lists/eldergleam-a-next-gen-modlist-4-0-2); [Licentia NEXT](https://loadorderlibrary.com/lists/licentia-next); [Halls](https://loadorderlibrary.com/lists/halls-of-sovngarde).
- **Version banner as the first separator:** seen in LoreRim, Elysium, Eldergleam, Licentia, Apostasy, Living Skyrim, Masterstroke, Tempus, NGVO, CSVP, Keizaal, Lost Legacy, Septimus, Aurora, NEFARAM, Wunduniik and Tahrovin, for example `Elysium Remastered v3.3.0` (in Living Skyrim it follows a `DLC` header) — see the sequences above.
- **Inbox separator at the bottom for new mods:** `NEWLY DOWNLOADED MODS (DO NOT LEAVE IT HERE!!)` (Eldergleam), `N E W - SORTING REQUIRED!` (GTS), `99. ___ NEW & UNSORTED ___` (Wunduniik), `ADDED MODS - Make your additions below this line` (Licentia), `Not Yet Installed…` (NGVO) — see the sequences above.

### Inferences
- Numbers work as stable labels and support the guide-to-pane mapping in Nolvus and Step. They carry a renumbering cost, shown by Wunduniik's out-of-order and duplicate numbers and by Licentia's jump to 98 and 99.
- Empty parents are the most common way of simulating nesting among high-separator lists. Nolvus shows that a decimal outline can do it with almost no empty separators (1 of 69).

### Gaps
- No list documents why it chose its scheme in the sources read, so the motives above are inferred from structure.
- An "empty" separator is either a deliberate header or an unused category. Gate to Sovngarde has many unused path entries, so its 59 is not all headers.

## Where do patches and outputs go?

### Takeaway
Patches are mostly spread out. Across 25 lists, a median of 82% of patch-named mods sit within six positions of a mod sharing a word in their name, and a median of only 36% fall in the last quarter of the pane. ## Evidence-based observations for the decision repository

### Takeaway
A defensible structure is a stable functional outline with a fixed end zone, a hybrid patch rule, and a default visual sequence from broad to specific. The evidence supports each of these as common practice, and it does not prove any of them best.

### Cited Findings
All figures below come from the per-list table and tables above; each list's source link is in those tables.
1. **Numbering is optional.** 18 of 24 SE lists do not number. Lists with numbers include some of the largest (Nolvus, Wunduniik) and lists without include others (LoreRim, Twisted).
2. **If numbering, expect drift.** Wunduniik has out-of-order top-level numbers and duplicates; Licentia reserves `98` and `99`; Step's guide starts at `02`.
3. **Size has no standard.** Separators range from 13 to 289, and mods per separator from about 7 (GTS) to about 52 (Halls of Sovngarde), with a median near 22.
4. **Nesting is simulated.** Empty parents (Apostasy, Licentia, Living Skyrim, LoreRim, Twisted), decimal labels (Nolvus), or path prefixes (GTS) are the three working forms.
5. **The end zone is the most consistent part:** late patches, then outputs, then ENB, maps and optional items, usually with an inbox separator at the bottom.
6. **Output order:** terrain LOD, TexGen, then DynDOLOD is close to universal; Synthesis placement varies.
7. **Patches:** the median list keeps 82% of patch-named mods near a related mod and 36% in the last quarter of the pane, while also having a late section. Wunduniik, Keizaal and Halls of Sovngarde are the centralized exceptions.
8. **Visual order:** nature, then structures, then objects, with particle lights late, is the majority pattern. Wunduniik and Living Skyrim show that other orders ship.
9. **Priority sections are explicit.** Early loader, late loader, finishing line and override separators are how lists express conflict priority without abandoning functional groups.
10. **Separators also carry operating instructions.** Version banners, `DO NOT DISABLE`, `ONLY PICK 1` and conflict-status tags are common, so the pane doubles as documentation.

### Inferences
- A sensible default for a new list: an unnumbered or lightly numbered functional outline, empty parents only where a section gets large, a hybrid patch rule, and a fixed, documented end zone. This is my reading of the common practice.
- Avoid citing "most lists do X" as independent confirmation, given the shared template families noted above.
- The middle-section order should be decided by the list's own conflicts, because the reference lists do not converge on one.

### Gaps
- This evidence shows what lists do, not what works best. No source here measures stability or visual results by structure.
- Viva Skyrim is unverified, and official files for Nolvus and Step were not found.
