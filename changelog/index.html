Unversioned (2026-11-02), save safety not assessed

Snapshot of the list: 161 mods under 23 separators.

  • Fixed: DLSS 5 Neural Rendering stopped working in Skyrim on 2026-11-03.

    • The cause: the owner ran Bethini (preset High, then recommended), which set bFull Screen=0 and bBorderless=1 in the profile’s SkyrimPrefs.ini. Community Shaders 1.9.1 ships Frame Generation on (frameGenerationMode=1), and it needs windowed mode, so exclusive fullscreen had kept it off without anyone noticing. In borderless mode it came on, and CS presented through its Direct3D 12 proxy, AMD FSR frame generation.
    • Why that broke NR: the RenoDX add-on serves Skyrim’s DLSS through its Direct3D 11 bridge only when the game presents through Direct3D 11. With the proxy it took the Direct3D 12 route, and no DLSS call reached it. ReShade.log shows NR-VERDICT state=NOT_ENGAGED, evals=0, for the whole session.
    • Evidence: Bethini’s backup and log, CommunityShaders.log (Frame Generation enabled, using D3D12 proxy), SSEDisplayTweaks.log and ReShade.log. A capture from 20:35, before Bethini ran, shows NR working on the compat runtime.
    • Not the cause: Effects 11 and the PI-CHO ENB, both disabled, and no file collisions next to the exe.
    • The fix, on 2026-11-04: CS Frame Generation is off (frameGenerationMode=0 in SKSE - Outputs), so the RenoDX route works again. The owner gets frame generation from NVIDIA Smooth Motion instead. Smooth Motion is a driver setting the owner turns on in the NVIDIA App, outside the list. The old SettingsUser.json and modlist.txt are backed up in C:\Modding\_cleanup-backup\2026-11-04-dlss5-smoothmotion.
    • Seen in game on 2026-11-04, and the owner calls it perfect. The setup is CS’s DLSS upscaling, DLSS 5 NR through ReShade, and Smooth Motion with VSync turned on in the NVIDIA control panel.
    • Also changed by the owner: VSync is off in SSE Display Tweaks (EnableVSync=false). Its 120 fps limit stays.
    • The log: ReShade.log shows api=d3d11 path=bridge and NR-VERDICT state=ENGAGED. NR ran on 99.99% of eligible frames, about 7,900 evaluations, while the owner switched it on and off with F6. The swap chain is 8-bit R8G8B8A8_UNORM and windowed.
    • Frame rate: the game itself presents at about 60 fps (16.6 ms a frame), and Smooth Motion adds its frames in the driver on top.

  • Added, then disabled: Community Shaders Neural Rendering Addon 1.2.1 (Nexus 197720) in Shaders, and a runtime mod made here, Community Shaders NR - Runtime compat 310.9.16. The add-on is an SKSE plugin meant to run NR inside Community Shaders, alongside CS’s Frame Generation. On this list it never started.

    • Why it failed: on 1.5.97 its own version check failed: Game compatibility check failed; no hooks installed: SKSE and the executable report different Skyrim versions (CommunityShadersNR.log). SKSE then reported it as incompatible. Its author tested only 1.7.104, on an RTX 5080.
    • Kept: both folders are kept, disabled. The runtime mod holds a copy of the compat nvngx_dlssnr.dll (sha256 cdfa6c1e…). The add-on’s INI has ResolutionPercent=67 and the overlay on. The DLSS 5 note has the details.

  • Disabled, then enabled again: DLSS 5 - ReShade and RenoDX, which was off while the add-on above was tried.

  • Added by the owner, recorded here: HDR - Community Shaders 1.2.2, Effects 11 1.0.0 and PI-CHO ENB N9.7 for Obsidian, all in Shaders and all disabled now. The ENB mod holds only preset files, with no ENB binary, and ENB doesn’t fit a Community Shaders list.

    • HDR: the owner found CS’s HDR output looked broken. It was disabled on 2026-11-04 to return to the 8-bit swap chain that DLSS 5 last worked on. The HDR mod makes the swap chain 10-bit even with HDR output off.

  • Changed by the owner through Bethini: besides the window mode, fGrassStartFadeDistance 40000 to 4096, iMaxDesired 10000 to 2000 and iNumFocusShadow 0 to 4, in SkyrimPrefs.ini. Not reverted. Bethini’s own game path points at the Steam copy, though it wrote only the profile’s INI.

  • Added, then disabled: Terrain Depth - Outputs, in Outputs, generated by the new tools/terrain_depth.py, and listed in rules/rules.json as an output mod. It deepened the PBR ground’s parallax toward the converted road’s, as the owner asked after a look in game. In game the ground smeared into streaks, so it was disabled the same day and the ground is back to TomatoRim’s own maps. The folder is kept. See the road edges note and 0015.

    • What it holds: 59 of the winning ground height maps, stretched around their own average toward a relief of 0.09, at most 3 times. Also 9 texture set files whose displacementScale rose toward 1.0 with the average re-centred: TomatoRim’s pine forest 02 (0.325) and Reach grass (0.627), and seven of Faultier’s Soul Cairn sets.
    • Why not raise the scales: height blending weighs layers by (height - 0.5) * displacementScale. Raising the scale alone would also lift the ground’s average and let it swallow the road’s edges and faint trails.
    • Conflicts on purpose: it wins over TomatoRim PBR Landscapes, TomatoRim PBR - Northern Roads, Faultier’s PBR Skyrim, Tomato’s Riften and Ratway and Vanaheimr Mines and Caves, on those files only.
    • Why it smeared: the files were as intended (averages kept, spread raised, every mip checked). TomatoRim’s height maps carry little real height detail, and a 1.6 to 3 times stretch made their small bumps too steep for the shader’s 16-step parallax march.

  • Changed: the converted Omen Apollo road’s displacementScale from 1.0 to 0.5, then back to 1.0 the same day. At 0.5 the owner found the road flat. 1.0 is the value TomatoRim’s own files give this set. The ground stays TomatoRim’s and the roads stay Northern Roads, rather than a move to Vanaheimr or back to Blended Roads (0015).
